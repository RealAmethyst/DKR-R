# Race cue sounds

Sounds for DKR-R's race sound cues (`runtime-recomp/src/game/race_cues`).
All are original sounds contributed to this fork by its maintainer, who owns
them, for use in DKR-R. They are not game assets.

- `track_edge.wav`: the track edge sound, a loop (PCM 16-bit mono, 44,100 Hz).
- `turn_signal.wav`: one beep of the turn signal, played three times
  (PCM 16-bit mono, 44,100 Hz).
- `beacon.wav`: the hub beacon, a loop played higher as the destination
  gets closer (PCM 16-bit mono, 44,100 Hz, 1.87 s).

The loader accepts 16-bit PCM WAV, mono or stereo, at any rate.
